Mike Lake spent his early career with the Edmonton Oilers, rising to national accounts manager, before winning federal office in 2006. He has represented a series of Edmonton-area ridings since — now Leduc—Wetaskiwin, which he took in 2025 with 74.7% of the vote, another of Alberta's safest seats — and served as Parliamentary Secretary to the Minister of Industry under Stephen Harper from 2008 to 2015.

He is best known across party lines for his autism advocacy, built around his son Jaden, who is autistic: he has spoken about it in the House for years and raised funds for autism research and awareness. That work is largely non-partisan and shows up in his committee and advocacy record rather than in whipped divisions, so the entries below test him on the standard Alberta Conservative file — taxes, pipelines, spending, accountability — where his votes track the platform, and note the one federalism vote he was paired on. The Voting Record section is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
