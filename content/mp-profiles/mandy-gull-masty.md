Mandy Gull-Masty represents the northern Quebec riding of Abitibi—Baie-James—Nunavik—Eeyou and serves as Minister of Indigenous Services in the Carney government. Her portfolio delivers programs and services to Indigenous communities. As a cabinet minister she votes with the government.
