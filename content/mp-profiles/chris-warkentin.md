Chris Warkentin has represented Grande Prairie (and its predecessor riding) since 2006, making him one of the longest-serving members of the entire House. Over nearly two decades he has held a range of senior caucus roles, the kind of institutional positions — whip, committee chair, House-tactics jobs — that give a member a granular feel for parliamentary procedure.

That longevity is the reason his record is a useful benchmark. A member who has spent his career inside the machinery of the Commons should be at his most reliable precisely on the procedural and fiscal votes where the Conservative case is sharpest — and his Alberta base keeps the energy and crime files close to home. His ballots below test whether the veteran votes the way the platform reads. The Voting Record section is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
