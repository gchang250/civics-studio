Kevin Lamoureux has held Winnipeg North since a 2010 byelection, making him one of Manitoba's longest-serving federal MPs. He previously sat in the Manitoba Legislative Assembly as a Liberal. He is the Liberal Party's Parliamentary Secretary to the Government House Leader and plays an active procedural role managing government legislation through the House.

Lamoureux votes with the Liberal government on virtually every bill. His profile is most useful as a window into how the government's legislative agenda plays out on the floor, and as a marker for Liberal positioning on immigration and Manitoba-specific issues.
