Martin Champoux, born in Quebec City in 1968, has represented Drummond in Quebec's Centre-du-Québec region since 2019, holding it in 2025 with 42.8% of the vote. He is the Bloc Québécois critic for heritage, arts, culture, secularism and Quebec values, and was elected vice-chair of the Standing Committee on Canadian Heritage in the 45th Parliament.

His is one of the more coherent portfolios in the caucus for a voting tracker, because culture and broadcasting policy — advertising rules, the language obligations of federal Crown corporations, the defence of Quebec's secularism framework — produces recorded votes that map onto the platform. Bill S-211 and Bill C-9 both fall inside it, and Bill C-245 is the identity plank his file is built to defend. The budget vote, as with the rest of the caucus, is discipline rather than portfolio.
