Gabriel Ste-Marie taught economics at the Cégep régional de Lanaudière and worked as a researcher at the Institut de recherche en économie contemporaine before winning Joliette in 2015. He has held the seat through every election since, taking 49.3% in 2025 — down nearly six points, but still close to an outright majority of votes cast. He has been the Bloc Québécois critic for finance and the treasury board since 2021, and in the 45th Parliament he was elected vice-chair of the Standing Committee on Industry, Science and Technology.

His parliamentary career includes an unusual interruption: he served briefly as Bloc House Leader in 2017–18 and resigned amid the caucus revolt against then-leader Martine Ouellet, leaving the caucus in February 2018 and rejoining that September. That history matters for reading his record, because it establishes that his party discipline is a choice rather than a reflex. The signature issue he has pressed longest — Canada's treatment of corporate income booked offshore — has not come to a recorded vote in this Parliament, so the entries below test him on the budget votes his portfolio actually owns.
