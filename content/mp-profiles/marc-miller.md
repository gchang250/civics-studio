Marc Miller represents the Montreal riding of Ville-Marie—Le Sud-Ouest—Île-des-Soeurs and serves as Minister of Canadian Identity and Culture, with responsibility for Official Languages, in the Carney government. His portfolio covers culture, heritage, and language, so votes on cultural protections best reflect his mandate. As a cabinet minister he votes with the government.
