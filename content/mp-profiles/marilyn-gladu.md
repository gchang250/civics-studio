Marilyn Gladu is a professional engineer — a Queen's University graduate who spent her career in Sarnia's petrochemical sector — first elected as a Conservative in 2015. She held Sarnia—Lambton—Bkejwanong in the 2025 election with 53.2% of the vote, running on the Conservative platform, and served over the years as the party's health critic, civil-liberties critic, and chair of the Status of Women committee.

Then, on 8 April 2026, she crossed the floor to the Liberals — the fourth Conservative to do so during the 45th Parliament — saying it was the "best thing" for herself and her riding, and that her experience could help deliver Mark Carney's agenda on housing, trade, defence and the cost of living. The move drew sharp reactions at home, and it carried a particular irony: just three months earlier, in January 2026, she had publicly backed a proposal to require by-elections when MPs switch parties, saying constituents "deserve a chance to have a redo." She got no such redo.

That is what makes her voting record worth reading closely, and why this tracker lists her by her current party rather than the one she was elected under. The ballots themselves show the crossing: through the winter she voted the Conservative line, and from mid-April onward she votes with the Liberal government — against fuel-tax cuts, for the budget, for Build Canada Homes. The entries below deliberately span both sides of April 2026. The Voting Record section is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
