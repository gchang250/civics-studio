Arnold Viersen is a journeyman auto mechanic by trade who has represented Peace River—Westlock, in northern Alberta, since 2015, winning it in 2025 with 77.1% — one of the very safest seats in the country. His record in the House is defined as much by social conservatism as by the standard Alberta economic file: he sponsored a 2016 motion that produced the first federal study of the public-health effects of online pornography since 1985, and he has been openly opposed to abortion, positions he describes as personal rather than party policy.

Those views belong in an honest account of him, and they shape how to read his votes, but the entries below stick to what the recorded divisions actually test: fuel taxes, pipelines, the budget, and the Criminal Code bills where his social conservatism and the Conservative whip point the same way. A 77% margin means he answers to a very safe riding, which tends to make a member's votes a purer expression of conviction than of electoral calculation.
