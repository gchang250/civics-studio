Anita Anand has represented the Oakville area since 2019 (now the riding of Oakville East) and has held a series of senior cabinet positions: Minister of Public Services and Procurement, Minister of National Defence, President of the Treasury Board, and later Transport and Industry. Under Mark Carney she was appointed Minister of Foreign Affairs in May 2025, the role she holds today. As defence minister she oversaw one of Canada's largest defence spending increases in decades and helped manage federal support for Ukraine.

Anand votes with the Liberal government as a cabinet minister. Her record is most informative on foreign-affairs, defence, and spending files where her portfolios have given her particular influence. The Voting Record section below is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
