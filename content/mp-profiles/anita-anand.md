Anita Anand has represented Oakville since 2019 and has held a series of senior cabinet positions, including Minister of National Defence and President of the Treasury Board. She became one of the most prominent female cabinet ministers of the Trudeau era, overseeing Canada's largest defence spending increase in decades and managing the federal government's Ukraine support. She was re-elected in 2025 and continues in cabinet under Mark Carney.
