Jill McKnight represents Delta, B.C. and serves as Minister of Veterans Affairs and Associate Minister of National Defence in the Carney government. Her portfolio focuses on services and benefits for veterans, so votes touching veterans and social supports are the clearest read on her mandate. As a cabinet minister she votes with the government.
