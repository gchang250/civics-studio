Louis Plamondon has held the same Quebec seat, now named Bécancour—Nicolet—Saurel—Alnôbak, since 1984, winning a 13th consecutive term in 2025. In November 2025 he was formally recognized as the longest continuously serving MP in Canadian history, surpassing Sir Wilfrid Laurier. Now in his 80s, Plamondon has said he intends to keep running for as long as his health holds out, and was applauded across party lines in the House for the milestone.
