Tim Uppal was first elected in Edmonton—Sherwood Park in 2008 and is now the MP for Edmonton Mill Woods after boundary redistributions. He served as Minister of State for Multiculturalism and Minister of State for Democratic Reform under Stephen Harper. He is one of the first Sikhs elected to the Canadian Parliament. He was re-elected in 2025 in his Edmonton-area riding.

Uppal is a reliable Conservative caucus vote with a particular focus on multiculturalism, immigration, and small-business issues drawn from his South Asian Canadian community ties.
