Gary Anandasangaree represents Scarborough—Guildwood—Rouge Park and serves as Minister of Public Safety in the Carney government. His portfolio covers policing, border security, and criminal-justice legislation, so his votes on crime and public-safety bills are the clearest read on his mandate. As a cabinet minister he votes with the government.
