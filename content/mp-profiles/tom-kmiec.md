Tom Kmiec has represented Calgary Shepard since the riding was created in 2015, winning it in 2025 with 68.0% — one of the largest margins in the country. He came up through Alberta policy circles, including the Calgary Chamber of Commerce, served as Conservative national caucus chair from 2019 to 2021, and held deputy critic roles in foreign affairs and finance. Since May 2025 he has served as Deputy Speaker of the House of Commons.

That last role matters for reading his record. A Deputy Speaker votes as an ordinary member when on the floor, but does not vote on business they are presiding over — so some gaps in his ballot record are procedural rather than political, and the entries below flag where that is the likely explanation. Away from the chair, his votes track the Conservative platform cleanly on taxes, spending and affordability. He is also known for cross-partisan advocacy on rare-disease treatment access and for human-rights work on persecuted minorities abroad, files that are more visible in his committee and private-member work than in the whipped divisions here.
