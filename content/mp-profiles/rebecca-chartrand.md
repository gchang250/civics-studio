Rebecca Chartrand represents the northern Manitoba riding of Churchill—Keewatinook Aski and serves as Minister of Northern and Arctic Affairs in the Carney government. Her portfolio covers northern development, infrastructure, and Arctic sovereignty. As a cabinet minister she votes with the government.
