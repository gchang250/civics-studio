Blake Desjarlais is a Canadian politician who was elected to the House of Commons in the 2021 federal election to represent Edmonton Griesbach as a member of the New Democratic Party (NDP). He is the first openly two-spirit Member of Parliament in Canadian history. Desjarlais lost his seat to Conservative Kerry Diotte in the 2025 federal election.

During his term in the 44th Parliament, Desjarlais served as a prominent voice on reconciliation, Indigenous welfare, and systemic racism in policing. His votes are documented here as a historical record of his legislative alignment during his time in the House.
