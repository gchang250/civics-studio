Blake Desjarlais is one of the youngest MPs in Parliament and the first openly two-spirit MP in Canadian history. He is Cree and a member of the Fishing Lake Métis Settlement in Alberta. He was first elected in 2021 and held Edmonton Griesbach through the 2025 election, one of the NDP's few Alberta survivors.

Desjarlais has become one of Parliament's most visible Indigenous voices, consistently pushing on reconciliation, child welfare, and systemic racism in the criminal justice system. His votes are worth watching not only as a party signal but as a window into how the House addresses (or fails to address) Indigenous rights.
