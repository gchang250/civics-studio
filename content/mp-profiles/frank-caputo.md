Frank Caputo was first elected in Kamloops—Thompson—Cariboo in 2021 and re-elected in 2025. He is a former Crown prosecutor with a focus on criminal justice reform and serves as the Conservative shadow critic for justice and attorney general. His background makes him one of the House's more substantively engaged voices on bail, sentencing, and criminal procedure, and he has occasionally found common ground with the government on crime bills even while opposing Liberal economic policy.

Caputo's voting record is notable because his criminal justice background leads him to occasionally vote Yes on government legislation that most of his caucus opposes. The Voting Record section below is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
