Larry Brock represents Brantford—Brant South—Six Nations and serves as the Conservative Shadow Minister for Justice and Attorney General. A former Crown prosecutor, he is the Official Opposition's lead critic on criminal-justice legislation; his votes follow the Conservative caucus line.
