Arielle Kayabaga came to Canada as a refugee from Burundi, served on London city council, and won London West for the Liberals in 2021. In the 45th Parliament she is Deputy House Leader of the Government, part of the small team that schedules the government's business and manages the daily mechanics of getting bills through the Commons.

That role makes her record unusually legible. A House leadership member is expected to be present and on-side for the votes that matter to the government's survival, and to carry the procedural motions that the opposition most resents — so her ballots are less about personal conviction than about whether the machinery held. The most revealing entries below are the ones where her job and the platform's democratic-reform rhetoric pull in different directions.
