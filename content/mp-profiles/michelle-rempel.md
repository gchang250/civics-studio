Michelle Rempel Garner has represented the Calgary riding now known as Calgary Nose Hill since 2015 and was re-elected in 2025 with 59% of the vote. Her campaign centred on the national Conservative platform (tax cuts, deficit reduction, and opposition to Liberal energy and climate policy) rather than a distinct personal platform, consistent with her long record as a prominent Alberta Conservative voice.
