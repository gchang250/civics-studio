Michelle Rempel Garner has represented the Calgary riding now known as Calgary Nose Hill since 2015 and was re-elected in 2025 with 59% of the vote. She is one of the Conservative caucus's highest-profile communicators and has served as shadow minister for immigration, health, and natural resources at various points. She is known for direct, outspoken commentary on social media and a willingness to push back publicly, including within her own party.

Her voting record tracks tightly with the Conservative caucus under Poilievre.
