Steven MacKinnon represents Gatineau and serves as Leader of the Government in the House of Commons and Minister of Transport in the Carney government. As House Leader he manages the government's day-to-day legislative agenda, so he is a near-constant Yes on government business. As a cabinet minister he votes with the government.
