Melissa Lantsman is the Conservative Party's co-deputy leader (alongside Tim Uppal) and has represented Thornhill since 2021. In the 2025 election she won with 66.4% of the vote, the highest share in the riding's history, and was deployed by the party to campaign in 41 other ridings, reflecting her role as a senior voice for the national Conservative platform rather than a candidate running primarily on riding-specific asks.

She is the first openly lesbian and first Jewish woman ever elected as a Conservative MP.
