Yasir Naqvi came to Canada as a teenager after his father was jailed for leading a pro-democracy demonstration in Pakistan, and the family settled in Niagara Falls. He built a career in Ontario provincial politics — MPP for Ottawa Centre from 2007, and Attorney General from 2016, the first Muslim and first visible-minority person to hold that office — before losing his provincial seat in 2018 and winning the federal riding in 2021. He held Ottawa Centre in 2025 with 62.7% of the vote, a commanding result.

His riding is the seat of the federal public service, which gives the Liberal defence of the bureaucracy a direct local stake, and his provincial background as Attorney General makes the criminal-justice votes the part of his record he is best equipped to own. He briefly served as a parliamentary secretary before resigning the role in 2023, and now sits as a senior backbencher. The entries below weight the justice and public-safety legislation accordingly.
