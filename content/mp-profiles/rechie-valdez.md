Rechie Valdez represents Mississauga—Streetsville and serves as Minister of Women and Gender Equality and Secretary of State for Small Business and Tourism in the Carney government. Her portfolio focuses on gender equality and support for families, so votes on those measures best reflect her mandate. As a cabinet minister she votes with the government.
