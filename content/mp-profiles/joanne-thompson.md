Joanne Thompson represents St. John's East and serves as Minister of Fisheries in the Carney government. Her Newfoundland base and portfolio tie her closely to the Atlantic fishery, so her votes on fisheries and coastal legislation are the most informative on her mandate. As a cabinet minister she votes with the government.
