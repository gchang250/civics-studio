Gord Johns has represented Courtenay—Alberni since 2015 and won his closest race yet in 2025, holding off a Conservative challenger by about five points. His campaign emphasized environmental, economic, and social justice priorities and reconciliation, and he pointed to a twelvefold increase in federal infrastructure investment in the riding — from roughly $1.7 million to $18 million annually — as evidence of his track record in office.
