Bardish Chagger's parents immigrated from Punjab to Waterloo in the 1970s, and she worked as a constituency assistant to the MP she would eventually succeed before winning the seat herself in 2015. She held Waterloo in 2025 with 59.5% of the vote. Her cabinet career was substantial: Minister of Small Business and Tourism from 2015, then Government House Leader from 2016 — the first woman to hold that post — and Minister of Diversity and Inclusion and Youth from 2019 to 2021.

She now sits as a backbencher, which is the usual fate that makes an ex-minister interesting to track: the whip's hold loosens, the incentive to differentiate grows, and yet the record almost always stays loyal. Her time running the government's business in the House gives the procedural vote below a particular resonance — she has been on the other side of exactly these Standing Orders fights. The entries otherwise test her on the government's core fiscal and housing agenda, where her votes are reliably with the government.
