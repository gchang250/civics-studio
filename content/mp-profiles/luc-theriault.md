Luc Thériault taught philosophy at Collège de Maisonneuve for eighteen years and holds a specialized diploma in bioethics, which is an unusually apt background for the file he carries. He sat in Quebec's National Assembly for the Parti Québécois from 2003 to 2007, was defeated, and returned to elected office federally in 2015 as the member for Montcalm. He took 46.6% there in 2025. Since 2021 he has been the Bloc Québécois critic for health, medical assistance in dying, and drug licensing.

The MAID file is where he is most distinctive: the Bloc has pushed harder than any other caucus for advance requests, the mechanism that would let someone consent to assisted death before losing capacity, and Quebec has legislated for it while federal law has not caught up. That promise has not produced a recorded division in the 45th Parliament, so his health record here rests on jurisdiction votes and on Bill S-233.
