Adam Chambers won Simcoe North for the Conservatives in 2021. Before politics he worked as a senior advisor to Jim Flaherty, the finance minister through the 2008 financial crisis, and later in the financial industry — a résumé that has made him one of the caucus's more fluent voices on fiscal and economic policy.

That is the lens for reading his record. An MP who trades on fiscal credibility is most accountable on the budget and spending votes, where the Conservative case against the government is at its most concrete. The question his ballots answer is whether he is consistently there to vote the balanced-budget line when the government's central money bills are actually on the floor.
