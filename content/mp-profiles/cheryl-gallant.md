Cheryl Gallant has a chemistry degree from Western and worked as a group insurance executive and office manager before she won the riding in 2000, becoming the first woman elected federally in Renfrew County. She has now won eight consecutive elections, taking 55.7% in 2025 under the riding's post-redistribution name, Algonquin—Renfrew—Pembroke. Her committee work has run through National Defence, Veterans' Affairs and Industry, and she served as deputy Opposition house leader in the 37th Parliament.

She is also among the most consistently controversial members of the Commons, widely described as one of its most vocal social conservatives, with a long trail of remarks she has been condemned for — including 2021 comments accusing the government of wanting to normalize sexual activity with children, and her promotion of "false flag" claims about the 2022 convoy protest. None of that is voting data, and the entries below do not try to litigate it. What they show is a member who votes her stated positions reliably on property rights, fuel costs and the Criminal Code, and whose late-session ballots are increasingly recorded as paired rather than cast.
