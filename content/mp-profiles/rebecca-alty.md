Rebecca Alty represents the Northwest Territories and serves as Minister of Crown-Indigenous Relations in the Carney government. Her portfolio covers treaty relationships, self-determination, and reconciliation. As a cabinet minister she votes with the government; the record below reflects broad government legislation rather than Indigenous-specific recorded divisions, which were limited in this session.
