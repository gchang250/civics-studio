Shafqat Ali represents Brampton—Chinguacousy Park and serves as President of the Treasury Board in the Carney government. His portfolio manages federal spending and public administration, so his votes on supply and appropriation bills are the clearest expression of his mandate. As a cabinet minister he votes with the government.
