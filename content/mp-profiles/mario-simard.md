Mario Simard is a former political-science lecturer and press secretary who won Jonquière, in the Saguenay, for the Bloc Québécois in 2019, unseating the incumbent New Democrat. The riding sits in a region built on aluminum, forestry and hydro power, where federal industrial and resource policy lands directly on local jobs.

His record is worth reading where the Bloc's two instincts meet: sovereigntist suspicion of federal power, and a regional economy that depends on getting industrial policy right. The major-projects legislation of this Parliament is exactly the place that tension surfaces — a nationalist caucus weighing fast-tracked federal authority against Quebec's jurisdiction and its own region's interests.
