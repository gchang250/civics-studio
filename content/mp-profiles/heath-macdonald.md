Heath MacDonald represents the Prince Edward Island riding of Malpeque and serves as Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food in the Carney government. His portfolio ties him to farm policy and the agri-food supply chain, so his votes on food-system legislation best reflect his mandate. As a cabinet minister he votes with the government.
