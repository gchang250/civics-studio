Andrew Scheer has represented Regina—Qu'Appelle since 2004 and has served as Conservative House Leader since Pierre Poilievre became party leader in 2022. He won re-election in 2025 with 64% of the vote on a platform emphasizing natural resources, agriculture, crime, and affordability, priorities consistent with his long record representing a Saskatchewan riding.

His most notable moment of the current Parliament came off the campaign trail: after Poilievre lost his own Ottawa-area seat on election night 2025, Scheer was chosen as interim Conservative leader, a role he held for roughly three months until Poilievre won a byelection in Battle River—Crowfoot and returned to the House as Leader of the Opposition.
