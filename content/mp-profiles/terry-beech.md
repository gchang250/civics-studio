Terry Beech was a tech entrepreneur — he founded a company that won a BC ventures competition and worked in investment — before winning the newly created Burnaby North—Seymour in 2015. He held it in 2025 with 59.1% of the vote, a significant increase over his earlier results. From 2023 to 2025 he served as Minister of Citizens' Services under Justin Trudeau, a portfolio built around improving federal service delivery, including a proposed 30-business-day guarantee for passports.

When Mark Carney formed his government in 2025, the Citizens' Services portfolio was dissolved and Beech was not offered another cabinet role, returning him to the backbench. That makes him one of several former ministers in this batch whose records test the same question: does an ex-minister keep voting for the government's program once the perks of office are gone? On the fiscal, housing and defence votes below, his answer is a consistent yes — in a Vancouver-area riding where the housing votes in particular carry real local weight.
