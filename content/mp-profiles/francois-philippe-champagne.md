François-Philippe Champagne has represented Saint-Maurice—Champlain since 2015. Widely seen as a possible contender in the 2025 Liberal leadership race after Justin Trudeau's resignation, he chose not to run, saying he wanted to focus on defending Canada from tariffs under the incoming Trump administration, and endorsed Mark Carney. Carney appointed him Minister of Finance when his cabinet was sworn in, a role he has kept (with National Revenue added) since the 2025 election.
