Chrystia Freeland served as Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister under Justin Trudeau before resigning from cabinet in December 2024 amid public disagreements over fiscal policy. She ran in the subsequent Liberal leadership race but lost to Mark Carney. She was re-elected in University—Rosedale in the 2025 federal election and serves in a senior role in the Carney government.
