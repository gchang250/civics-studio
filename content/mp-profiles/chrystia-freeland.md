Chrystia Freeland served as Canada's Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance under Justin Trudeau. She represented University—Rosedale as a Liberal MP in the 44th Parliament. Following the 2025 federal election, she served briefly in the 45th Parliament before resigning her seat in early 2026.

As a senior cabinet minister, Freeland's voting record represents a core expression of the government's legislative agenda. Her votes in the House of Commons are documented here as a historical record.
