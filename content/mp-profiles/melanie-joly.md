Mélanie Joly has represented Ahuntsic-Cartierville since 2015 and posted her strongest result yet in the 2025 election, winning 61% of the vote. Like several senior cabinet colleagues, she opted out of the 2025 Liberal leadership race, citing the need to focus on the tariff threat from the incoming Trump administration in her then-role as Foreign Affairs Minister, and endorsed Mark Carney. After the election, Carney moved her to Industry, with responsibility for economic development in Quebec.
