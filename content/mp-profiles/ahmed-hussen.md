Ahmed Hussen was born in Mogadishu, fled the Somali civil war at 15, arrived in Canada as a refugee, and became a lawyer and then, in 2015, the first Somali-Canadian elected to the House of Commons. He held York South—Weston—Etobicoke in 2025 with 55.3% of the vote. His ministerial career spanned four consecutive portfolios under Justin Trudeau: Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship; Families, Children and Social Development; Housing and Diversity and Inclusion; and International Development.

He is not in Mark Carney's cabinet; in the 45th Parliament he was elected chair of the Standing Committee on Foreign Affairs and International Development, a natural landing spot given his last portfolio. An MP who has held immigration, housing and development files brings unusual first-hand knowledge to exactly the legislation this Parliament is voting on — which makes the entries below, on housing, citizenship and the budget, a test of whether a former minister keeps backing the programs he used to run. He does. The Voting Record section is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
