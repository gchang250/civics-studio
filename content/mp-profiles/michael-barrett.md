Michael Barrett grew up in Arnprior, served in the Canadian Armed Forces as a telecommunications lineman, worked as a human resources manager, and spent four years as a councillor in Edwardsburgh/Cardinal before winning a by-election in December 2018. He has held Leeds—Grenville—Thousand Islands—Rideau Lakes in every general election since, taking 50.1% in 2025.

He has been the Conservatives' ethics attack dog for most of a decade: questioning ministers over SNC-Lavalin in 2019, the Prime Minister over WE Charity in 2020, and driving the ArriveCAN inquiry that put a two-person IT contractor at the centre of a national procurement scandal. Poilievre appointed him Shadow Minister for Ethics and Accountable Government in 2022; in July 2026 he moved to Veterans Affairs. That is a useful moment to audit the record, because an accountability portfolio produces votes that are easy to check — concurrence motions and opposition days where the House either backs the scrutiny or doesn't.
