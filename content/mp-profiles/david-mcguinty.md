David McGuinty represents Ottawa South and serves as Minister of National Defence in the Carney government. His portfolio makes him responsible for the Canadian Armed Forces and the government's defence agenda, so his votes on the National Defence Act (Bill C-11) are the most direct expression of his mandate. As a cabinet minister he votes with the government.
