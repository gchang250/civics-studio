Kody Blois has represented the rural Nova Scotia riding of Kings—Hants since 2019 and secured a third consecutive term in 2025, winning 61% of the vote on a campaign built around agriculture — his signs read "Farmers for Kody" without party branding, emphasizing his personal standing with local farmers. He served as Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food and Rural Economic Development from March to May 2025 before a cabinet reshuffle, and now serves as parliamentary secretary to the prime minister.
