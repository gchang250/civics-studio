Greg McLean won Calgary Centre in the 2019 election, flipping a seat that had been Liberal-held for nearly a decade. He was re-elected in both 2021 and 2025. As the MP for downtown Calgary, McLean is one of the few Conservative members representing a genuinely urban mixed riding, though the seat leans heavily Conservative.

McLean serves on the Standing Committee on Finance and is a reliable Conservative caucus voice on fiscal and energy policy.
