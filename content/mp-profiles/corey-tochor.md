Corey Tochor has represented Saskatoon—University for the Conservatives since 2019, part of the near-solid bloc of Saskatchewan seats the party holds. His politics sit firmly in the Prairie conservative tradition, where opposition to federal carbon pricing and support for the energy sector are less policy preferences than the baseline expectation of the electorate.

For a member from that province, the platform's energy and carbon-tax commitments are the votes that matter most to his constituents, and they are the easiest place to check whether the rhetoric of the campaign survives contact with the order paper. His record below is a clean test of Prairie-Conservative discipline on the fuel, emissions and pipeline questions his region cares about — and on the budget votes that decide a minority Parliament.
