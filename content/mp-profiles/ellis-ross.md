Ellis Ross represents the northwest B.C. riding of Skeena—Bulkley Valley and serves as the Conservative Shadow Minister for Environment and Climate Change. A former chief councillor of the Haisla Nation associated with LNG development, he is a strong resource-development voice; his votes follow the Conservative caucus line on carbon pricing and energy.
