Bob Zimmer has represented the sprawling northern British Columbia riding of Prince George—Peace River—Northern Rockies since 2011, one of the longer-serving members of the Conservative caucus. His territory is resource country — forestry, oil and gas, mining — and his politics have tracked those industries throughout.

For an MP from a region defined by resource extraction and long driving distances, the platform's energy and fuel-tax commitments are not abstractions; they are constituency economics. His record is worth reading as a straightforward test of whether a veteran resource-riding Conservative votes the pipeline, carbon-tax and spending lines his region cares about most, on the specific motions where those questions came to the floor.
