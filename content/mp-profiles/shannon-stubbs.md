Shannon Stubbs has represented the oil-and-gas-heavy Lakeland riding in northern Alberta since 2015. She is one of the Conservative caucus's most prominent voices on energy and natural resources, and served as shadow critic for natural resources under multiple Conservative leaders. Lakeland is one of Canada's safest Conservative ridings, spanning the oilsands region between Edmonton and Fort McMurray.

Stubbs votes squarely with the Conservative caucus on energy, environment, and fiscal matters. Her votes are a reliable indicator of where the official opposition stands on resource-sector legislation and any bill touching on carbon pricing.
