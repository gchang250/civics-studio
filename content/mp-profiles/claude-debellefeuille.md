Claude DeBellefeuille was a social worker before she was a politician. She first won Beauharnois—Salaberry in 2006, lost the seat to the NDP in the 2011 orange wave, sat out two Parliaments, and came back in 2019. She has served as the Bloc Québécois Chief Whip since 2021 — a role she had also filled briefly in 2010–11 — and held her seat in 2025 with 43.9% of the vote, down about four and a half points.

A whip is an unusual subject for a voting-record tracker, because the job is to produce other people's votes rather than to cast interesting ones of her own. That makes her record most legible on the votes where the Bloc stood alone: Bill C-245, which drew 22 supporters in a 338-seat House, is a better measure of her work than any motion that passed with everyone's support. It also makes her absences more conspicuous than a backbencher's would be. Her riding sits in the Montérégie farm belt, which gives the Bloc's supply-management commitments a direct local stake.
