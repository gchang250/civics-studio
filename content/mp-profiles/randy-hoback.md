Randy Hoback has represented Prince Albert since 2008 and was re-elected in 2025 with 71.6% of the vote, nearly 20,000 votes ahead of his Liberal challenger. He has served as Shadow Minister for International Trade and chair of the Saskatchewan Conservative caucus, and campaigned in 2025 on economic growth, support for local industry, and tackling crime.
