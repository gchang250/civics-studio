John Williamson came to politics from the small-government movement: an editorial writer at the National Post, then federal director of the Canadian Taxpayers Federation, then director of communications in Stephen Harper's PMO before winning a New Brunswick seat in 2011. After a defeat and a return, he now represents Saint John—St. Croix, which he took in 2025 with 53.1% of the vote. Since 2022 he has chaired the House of Commons Standing Committee on Public Accounts, and he was re-elected to that chair in June 2025.

Chairing Public Accounts is the fact that makes his record worth reading, because that committee is where the ArriveCAN procurement scandal was dissected, and its reports reach the floor as concurrence votes the government resists. An MP who has spent his career arguing for value-for-money in public spending, and who now chairs the committee that audits it, can be checked against votes he helped bring to the House rather than ones he merely attended.
