John Barlow represents the Alberta riding of Foothills and serves as the Conservative Shadow Minister for Agriculture and Agri-Food, the Official Opposition's lead voice for farmers and ranchers. His votes follow the Conservative caucus line on carbon pricing and fiscal policy.
