Marie-Hélène Gaudreau has represented Laurentides—Labelle, north of Montreal, for the Bloc Québécois since 2019. Within the caucus she has worked the democratic-institutions and ethics file — the accountability beat, where the job is scrutinizing how the government conducts itself rather than advancing a marquee policy of her own.

That makes her record a clean test of consistency. An MP whose portfolio is the integrity of Parliament is most accountable when the rules of the House are actually being rewritten, and this Parliament handed her exactly that vote. Alongside it, her ballots track the Bloc's core commitments on the fiscal imbalance, Quebec's jurisdiction and consumer protection, plus the regional grievances of the Laurentians.
