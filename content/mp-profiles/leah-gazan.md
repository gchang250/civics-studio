Leah Gazan is a Wood Mountain Lakota activist and scholar who has represented Winnipeg Centre since 2019. She is one of the NDP's most progressive members, and one of the first Indigenous women elected to Parliament from Manitoba. She survived the 2025 election in one of Canada's most impoverished urban ridings. She has been one of the most outspoken parliamentary advocates for a guaranteed basic income and for MMIWG justice.

Gazan's votes are worth tracking because she pushes from the left even within the NDP caucus, and her riding's demographics make her particularly attuned to poverty, housing, and Indigenous welfare issues. The Voting Record section below is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
