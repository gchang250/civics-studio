Leah Gazan has represented Winnipeg Centre since 2019 and won re-election in 2025 with 39% of the vote. She has built her time in Parliament around a national framework for a Guaranteed Livable Basic Income (Bill C-223), alongside affordable housing, healthcare, fair wages, and women's and Indigenous rights.
