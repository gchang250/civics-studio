Mario Beaulieu has represented La Pointe-de-l'Île since 2015 and won re-election in 2025 with 43% of the vote. He serves as the Bloc Québécois's spokesperson on Official Languages and has spent his career advocating for the primacy of French in Quebec, including a past bill (C-421) that would have made sufficient French knowledge a condition for Quebec citizenship applications, and calls for greater use of French in scientific research and publishing.
