Scott Aitchison represents Parry Sound—Muskoka and serves as the Conservative Shadow Minister for Housing. A former mayor of Huntsville, he is the Official Opposition's lead critic on housing policy, favouring deregulation and supply-side reform over government-built housing; his votes follow the Conservative caucus line.
