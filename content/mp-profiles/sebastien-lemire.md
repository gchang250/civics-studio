Sébastien Lemire has represented Abitibi—Témiscamingue — a vast mining, forestry and farming region in Quebec's northwest — since 2019, and held it in 2025 with 49.4% of the vote. He is the Bloc Québécois critic for industry, regional high-speed internet and entrepreneurship, and in the 45th Parliament was elected vice-chair of both the Standing Committee on Indigenous and Northern Affairs and the Standing Committee on Public Accounts.

His file is a good fit for a remote riding: rural connectivity, regional economic development, and the durability of the goods people far from big-city repair shops depend on. That last point turns the Bloc's right-to-repair plank into a vote with real local logic behind it, and Bill C-267 is where it landed. The entries below test him on the industry and regional votes his portfolio owns, and flag the budget vote as the caucus-discipline exercise it is.
