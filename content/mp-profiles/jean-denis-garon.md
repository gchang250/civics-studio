Jean-Denis Garon is an economist who taught at UQAM's School of Management Sciences before winning Mirabel for the Bloc Québécois in 2021. He held the riding in 2025 with 39.9% of the vote — a solid win, but nearly seven and a half points down from his first, with the Liberals closing to within about 3,700 votes. In the 45th Parliament he serves as vice-chair of the House of Commons Standing Committee on Finance, which makes him one of the few MPs whose day job matches his training.

His votes are worth tracking for two reasons. The first is technical: an academic economist inside a sovereigntist caucus has to reconcile textbook fiscal analysis with a party line built on jurisdiction, and the budget votes are where that reconciliation either happens or doesn't. The second is local. Mirabel carries a specific historical wound — the mass expropriation of farmland for an airport that never grew into the hub it was meant to be — and in February 2026 the House voted unanimously on his party's motion demanding an apology for it.
