Xavier Barsalou-Duval was an auditor and teacher, and president of the Bloc's youth wing from 2011 to 2015, before he won Pierre-Boucher—Les Patriotes—Verchères in 2015 with the lowest vote share of any MP elected that year — 28.6%, on a badly split ballot. He has consolidated the riding since, taking 46.1% in 2025. He served as the Bloc's critic for transport, infrastructure and communities in the 44th Parliament.

Two things make his record unusually testable. First, he is a bill sponsor, not just a voter: Bill C-245, the Bloc's attempt to lift the federal Multiculturalism Act out of Quebec, is his, which turns one of the caucus's identity commitments into a vote with his name on it. Second, the transport file produces recorded votes that map cleanly onto platform text — the electric-vehicle timeline, fuel taxes, the Clean Fuel Standard — where many portfolios in this Parliament produce almost none.
