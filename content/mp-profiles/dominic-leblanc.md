Dominic LeBlanc has represented Beauséjour, New Brunswick since 1998 and was re-elected to a ninth term in 2025 with 61% of the vote. During the campaign he served as Intergovernmental Affairs and Trade Minister amid the ongoing tariff dispute with the United States; after the election, Carney named him President of the King's Privy Council and Minister responsible for Canada-U.S. Trade, Intergovernmental Affairs, and building "one Canadian economy" by removing barriers to interprovincial trade.
