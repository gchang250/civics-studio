Lori Idlout is an Inuk lawyer, born in Igloolik in 1974, with a psychology degree from Lakehead and a JD from the University of Ottawa. She was executive director of the Nunavut Embrace Life Council from 2004 to 2011, working on suicide prevention and mental health, and later ran her own firm, Qusugaq Law, representing groups opposing the Baffinland iron mine expansion. She won the NDP nomination for Nunavut by coin toss — a first for the territory — and took the seat in 2021.

In April 2025 she held it by 41 votes: 2,853 to Liberal candidate Kilikvak Kabloona's 2,812, in a riding that covers a fifth of Canada's land mass. In March 2026 she crossed the floor to the Liberals, saying she had come to feel she was betraying her constituents by staying in the NDP caucus. She was the fourth MP to join the Liberal caucus during the 45th Parliament and the first from the New Democrats.

That makes her one of the most interesting records in the House to read, because the ballots themselves show the change. The parliamentary record dates her Liberal membership from 11 March 2026, and her votes before and after that line up on opposite sides of the government on budgets, housing and procedural motions. The entries below deliberately span both periods. The Voting Record section is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
