Sean Fraser, a former minister of immigration and of housing, initially announced he would not run again before reversing that decision and winning re-election in Central Nova, Nova Scotia, with 52% of the vote in 2025. He was subsequently sworn in as Minister of Justice and Attorney General, with responsibility for the Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency.

As a cabinet minister, Fraser is bound by cabinet solidarity — a stricter form of party discipline than an ordinary backbencher faces — so his voting record is a useful baseline for what "full whip alignment" looks like in the data below, against which more independent-minded backbench MPs can be compared.
