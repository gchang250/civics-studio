Ginette Petitpas Taylor has held Moncton—Dieppe (and its predecessor) since 2015, and is one of the more experienced hands in the New Brunswick Liberal caucus. She served as Minister of Health from 2017 to 2019 and later held other senior portfolios, including Official Languages — a natural fit for an Acadian member representing a bilingual region.

Her value as a case study is in the plainness of the record. She is not a firebrand or a portfolio-holder chasing a signature file; she is a veteran government MP, and the interesting question is simply whether the votes match the seniority — whether she is where the government needs her on the budget bills that decide a minority Parliament's survival, and on the affordability and housing measures her party campaigned on.
