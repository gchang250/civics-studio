Adam van Koeverden is an Olympic gold-medal sprint kayaker — K-1 500m champion in Athens in 2004 — and a two-time world champion who was first elected in the old riding of Milton in 2019. He now represents Burlington North—Milton West and, since 2025, serves as Secretary of State for Sport in the Carney ministry, a junior post that puts one of Canada's best-known athletes in charge of the file he knows best.

That makes his record a specific kind of test. A celebrity recruit with a narrow portfolio rarely drives legislation, so the honest question is whether he shows up as a reliable vote for the government's core agenda — housing, trade, affordability — and whether his own sport file ever actually reaches the floor.
