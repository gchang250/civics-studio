Christine Normandin represents Saint-Jean, Quebec, and was re-elected in 2025 by 1,260 votes over her Liberal challenger. Beyond her constituency work, her most notable role is institutional: she serves as the Bloc Québécois's Parliamentary Leader, coordinating the caucus's position and schedule in the House, and sits as vice-chair of the Standing Committee on Procedure and House Affairs. Detailed personal riding-level platform commitments beyond her role and re-election are not well documented in public reporting.
