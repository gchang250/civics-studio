Greg Fergus was a Liberal Party insider — staffer to cabinet ministers, then national director of the party under Stéphane Dion — before winning Hull—Aylmer, across the river from Ottawa, in 2015. He held the seat in 2025 with 62.1% of the vote. In October 2023 he was elected the 38th Speaker of the House of Commons, the first Black person to hold the office, a tenure that included ejecting Pierre Poilievre from the chamber in 2024 for calling the Prime Minister "wacko."

His speakership ended with the new Parliament: in May 2025 he ran for re-election as Speaker and lost to fellow Liberal Francis Scarpaleggia, returning him to the government benches as an ordinary MP. That transition is what makes his record legible again — as Speaker he voted only to break ties and stayed publicly neutral, so his return to normal voting in the 45th Parliament is the first clear read on his own positions in years. His riding, like Naqvi's, is heavily populated by federal public servants.
