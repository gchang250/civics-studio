John Brassard spent his working life as a firefighter and then a Barrie city councillor before winning the riding federally in 2015. He took Barrie South—Innisfil in 2025 with 57.8%, a substantial increase over his previous result. He served as Deputy Opposition Whip from 2017 to 2020, as Shadow Minister for Veterans Affairs before that, and as Opposition House Leader under Candice Bergen in 2022. In the 45th Parliament he chairs the Standing Committee on Access to Information, Privacy and Ethics.

Chairing ETHI is the fact that makes his record worth reading closely. The committee is where procurement scandals, lobbying disputes and conflict-of-interest questions get worked over, and its reports reach the floor as concurrence motions that the government generally resists. An opposition MP who chairs it has an unusual amount of control over what the House is forced to vote on — which means the accountability commitments in his platform can be checked against votes he helped create, not just votes he happened to be present for.
