Jenny Kwan has represented Vancouver East since 2015 and was re-elected in 2025 with 44% of the vote. Her platform centres on defending public healthcare and expanding dental and pharmacare coverage, building deeply affordable social and co-op housing, preventing large corporate landlords from buying up affordable housing stock, and tax fairness for the wealthiest earners.
