Jenny Kwan has represented Vancouver East since 1997, first in the BC Legislative Assembly and in the House of Commons since 2015. She is one of the most senior NDP MPs and has served as the party's housing and immigration critic. She held her seat through the 2025 NDP collapse that eliminated the party's official status.

Kwan is one of Parliament's most consistent voices on housing, refugee rights, and the overdose crisis. Her votes are worth tracking precisely because she has been willing to push back on government housing proposals she considers inadequate.
