Jenna Sudds was an Ottawa city councillor for Kanata North before winning the federal riding of Kanata in 2021. From 2023 to 2025 she served as Minister of Families, Children and Social Development, the department that administers the national early learning and child care agreements — the $10-a-day program that is among the Liberals' most concrete social-policy legacies.

Returning to the backbench after holding a cabinet portfolio makes her a useful case study: does a former families minister keep voting the family and affordability file when she no longer owns it, and does she remain a dependable government vote on the harder fiscal and trade questions?
