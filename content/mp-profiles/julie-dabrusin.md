Julie Dabrusin represents Toronto—Danforth and serves as Minister of the Environment, Climate Change and Nature in the Carney government. Her portfolio makes carbon pricing and emissions policy central to her mandate, so her votes on those files are the clearest expression of it. As a cabinet minister she votes with the government.
