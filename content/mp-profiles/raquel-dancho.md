Raquel Dancho has represented Kildonan—St. Paul since 2019 and held her seat in the 2025 election by 1,556 votes over her Liberal challenger. She has built her record around public safety, calling for stricter bail laws targeting repeat violent offenders and sponsoring Bill S-205 to support victims of intimate partner violence, while serving as the Conservatives' Shadow Minister for Public Safety and, most recently, Shadow Minister for Industry.
