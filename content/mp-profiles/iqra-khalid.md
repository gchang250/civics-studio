Iqra Khalid has represented Mississauga—Erin Mills since 2015, winning it in four consecutive elections. She is best known nationally for M-103, the 2017 motion condemning Islamophobia and religious discrimination that she brought to the House; it passed but drew a wave of hostility that made her one of the more recognizable backbenchers of her era.

That history is the reason her record is worth reading closely. An MP defined by a single motion on hate is exactly the person whose consistency can be tested when hate-crime legislation actually reaches the floor — and in this Parliament Bill C-9 did, more than once. The rest of her ballots track the Liberal agenda on housing, affordability and the fiscal plan. The Voting Record section below is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
