Brad Vis was first elected in 2019 in the old riding of Mission—Matsqui—Fraser Canyon and now represents Mission—Matsqui—Abbotsford in British Columbia's Fraser Valley. He holds degrees in political science from UBC and Carleton and worked in government and agri-business before Parliament, and he has become one of the Conservative caucus's most active voices on housing policy.

That focus is what makes his record interesting, and slightly awkward to read at first glance: a housing critic who votes against the government's housing bills. The tension is real but coherent — the Conservative position is that Ottawa should cut the taxes and permitting delays that choke supply rather than stand up a new federal builder. His ballots below let you judge whether the votes hold together with that argument, or just read as opposition for its own sake. The Voting Record section is drawn from Parliament's live vote data.
