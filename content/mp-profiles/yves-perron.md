Yves Perron has represented Berthier—Maskinongé, a farming riding along the St. Lawrence northeast of Montreal, since 2019 — he first ran in 2015 and finished second before winning on his second attempt. He held the seat in 2025 with 35.0% of the vote on a badly split ballot. He has been president of the Bloc Québécois since 2018, and since 2021 the party's critic for agriculture, agri-food and supply management; in the 45th Parliament he was elected vice-chair of the Standing Committee on Agriculture and Agri-Food.

Supply management — the system of quotas and tariffs protecting Quebec's dairy, poultry and egg producers — is the issue he is most identified with, and the Bloc made a protective bill its first priority of the Parliament. The honest difficulty for a tracker is that the marquee supply-management legislation did not produce a clean stand-alone division in session 45-1, so his agricultural convictions can't be tested against a single decisive ballot here. The entries below are candid about that: they lean on the jurisdiction and identity votes that did happen, and mark the manufacturing motion as a proxy rather than a direct hit.
