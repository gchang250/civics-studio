Joël Lightbound represents the Quebec City-area riding of Louis-Hébert and serves as Minister of Government Transformation, Public Services and Procurement in the Carney government. His portfolio covers federal service delivery and procurement, so his votes on supply and administration measures best reflect his mandate. As a cabinet minister he votes with the government.
