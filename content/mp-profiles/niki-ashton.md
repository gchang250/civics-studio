Niki Ashton has represented the vast northern Manitoba riding of Churchill—Keewatinook Aski since 2008. She ran for the NDP federal leadership twice before the 2025 election. She was one of the few NDP MPs to hold her seat in the 2025 election collapse and is among the most consistently progressive voices remaining in the House. She has been a vocal critic of both Liberal and Conservative governments on Indigenous rights, climate, and social spending.

As one of the NDP's remaining MPs after the party lost official status, Ashton votes as both a party representative and an independent left voice. Her votes often carry symbolic weight because the NDP caucus is too small to form a bloc, meaning her individual positions matter more than party-line counting would suggest.
