Niki Ashton served as the Member of Parliament for Churchill—Keewatinook Aski from 2008 until 2025. She has been one of the most prominent progressive voices in the federal NDP caucus, serving as shadow critic for jobs, training, and workforce development. Ashton lost her seat to Liberal Rebecca Chartrand in the 2025 federal election.

Throughout her tenure, Ashton advocated for Indigenous rights, public investment in northern infrastructure, and democratic reform. Her voting record in the 44th Parliament is documented here as a historical record of her legislative alignment.
