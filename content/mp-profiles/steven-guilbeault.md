Steven Guilbeault has represented Laurier—Sainte-Marie since 2019 and served as Canada's Minister of Environment and Climate Change from 2021 to 2025, later moving to Canadian Identity and Culture. A longtime environmental activist before entering politics, he left cabinet in November 2025 after the Carney government struck an energy deal with Alberta that included a memorandum of understanding toward a new bitumen pipeline. In May 2026, he announced he would resign his seat over the summer, saying he had "come to the conclusion that it is time for me to pursue my fight for environmental protection and the fight against climate change in a different way."
