Maninder Sidhu represents Brampton East and serves as Minister of International Trade in the Carney government. His portfolio puts him at the centre of Canada's response to the U.S. tariff dispute and its push to diversify trade, so his votes on trade legislation best reflect his mandate. As a cabinet minister he votes with the government.
